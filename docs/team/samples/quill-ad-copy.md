# Sample: Quill (Copywriter) - Meta Ad Variants

> Illustrative ad copy for an example product. Five distinct angles to test, not five rewrites of one idea. Each tests a different hypothesis so Dex can learn what message pulls.

**Angle 1: Pain-led** (hypothesis: the problem is felt strongly enough to stop the scroll)
Primary text: You didn't start a business to spend your evenings wrestling spreadsheets. That's exactly the part we take off your plate. Set it up once and get your nights back.
Headline: Stop doing this by hand
CTA: Learn more

**Angle 2: Outcome-led** (hypothesis: people buy the after-state, not the tool)
Primary text: Picture Monday morning with the week's numbers already done, already accurate, waiting for you. No catch-up, no cleanup. That's the default here.
Headline: Your week, already handled
CTA: Start free trial

**Angle 3: Social proof** (hypothesis: trust is the blocker, not understanding)
Primary text: Over 4,000 small teams switched last year and stopped looking back. The most common thing they tell us: "I wish I'd done it sooner."
Headline: Join 4,000+ teams who switched
CTA: See why

**Angle 4: Contrarian** (hypothesis: challenging the status quo earns attention)
Primary text: Most tools in this space are built for enterprises and priced for them too. We built the opposite: serious capability, sized and priced for a small team that actually has to use it.
Headline: Built for you, not the Fortune 500
CTA: Learn more

**Angle 5: Urgency / specific offer** (hypothesis: a concrete reason to act now lifts CTR)
Primary text: Setup takes about ten minutes and the first two weeks are free, no card needed. If it isn't saving you time by day three, walk away. You're not locked into anything.
Headline: Free for 14 days, no card
CTA: Start free trial

**Notes for Dex:** these test five different beliefs about why someone does or doesn't buy. Run them as separate ads in one ad set, give each a fair sample before judging, and read by hook rate first (did the first line stop the scroll) then by cost per trial. Once a winning angle emerges, I'll write three fresh variations within that angle to scale it. A couple of these make a soft time-saving claim and a "4,000 teams" stat; confirm both are true before this goes live, and I'll adjust any that aren't.

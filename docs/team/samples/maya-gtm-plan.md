# Sample: Maya (CMO) — 30-Day Go-To-Market Plan

> Illustrative deliverable for an example B2B SaaS launching a new product. Shows the format and thinking, not real numbers.

Here's how I'd frame the launch. The goal isn't "get attention," it's **100 qualified trials in 30 days at a blended CAC under $120**, because that's the threshold where the unit economics work given our $39/mo plan and current trial-to-paid rate. Everything below ladders up to that one number.

Three levers matter, and nothing else does this month. First, **warm demand**: your existing list and the founders' networks are the cheapest trials you'll ever get, so we squeeze those before spending a dollar on ads. Second, **paid prospecting on one channel**, not four. We pick the channel where the buyer is actually in a buying mindset and go deep enough to learn something. Third, **a launch moment** that concentrates attention into a single week instead of dribbling it out.

For ownership: I'd put **Dex** on a single Meta prospecting campaign at $80/day with three audiences and a hard read at day 14, because spreading $2.4k across four platforms teaches us nothing. **Quill** writes the launch announcement, three ad angles, and the activation email sequence. **Scout** pulls the live positioning and pricing of our two closest competitors this week so our messaging is sharper than theirs, not a louder version of the same claim. **Nova** stands up a simple trial funnel dashboard before we spend, because if we can't see where trials drop, we're flying blind. **Theo** runs a launch-week content series, and **Iris** produces the actual LinkedIn and Instagram posts with visuals.

The shape of the month: week 1 is foundations (tracking live, competitor intel in hand, copy drafted, list warmed). Week 2 we launch publicly and turn on paid. Week 3 we read the data and cut whatever's losing. Week 4 we scale the one thing that's working and write the retro.

Honest caveat: 100 trials in 30 days on a $2.4k budget is achievable only if the warm list pulls its weight, roughly half of these. If paid has to carry the whole number from a cold start, the realistic figure is closer to 50 to 70, and we should set that expectation with Andy now rather than explain a miss later.

**Next action, this week:** Nova confirms trial tracking is firing end to end, and Scout delivers the two-competitor teardown by Thursday. Once both land, Dex and Quill build the campaign. I'll check the funnel numbers with you every Monday.

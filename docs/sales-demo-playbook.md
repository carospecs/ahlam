# Ahlam sales demo and pitch playbook

Updated: September 20, 2026

Use this with the public demo page at `https://ahlam.io/demo` (local route: `/demo`). The goal is not to promise that Ahlam will magically increase sales. The goal is to show how Ahlam gets more verified inventory online, gives every approved part more chances to be found, and measures the buyer response.

## The one-sentence pitch

> Ahlam turns photos of a vehicle into editable used-parts listings, helps you price and organize them, and prepares them for your shop page, eBay, Facebook, and OfferUp—while you keep the final say before anything is published.

## The ten-minute in-person demo

### Minute 0–1: diagnose before presenting

Ask:

1. How many vehicles do you process in a normal month?
2. Where do you list parts today?
3. What stops more of your inventory from getting online—time, staffing, knowing the part, pricing, or photos?
4. Which parts do you most want to move this month?

Repeat the problem back in one sentence. Example: “So the parts are here, but one employee cannot photograph, research, and type enough listings to keep up.”

### Minute 1–4: use their vehicle or part

1. Photograph several useful angles and the VIN plate when available.
2. Run the AI scan.
3. Show the proposed part names, condition grades, descriptions, and starting prices.
4. Say clearly: “These are starting points, not facts you have to accept.”

### Minute 4–6: correct something together

Change a part name, fitment detail, grade, description, or price with the owner. This is important: it proves the shop remains in control and prevents the AI from looking like a black box.

### Minute 6–8: show the buyer channels

- Ahlam storefront: approved inventory appears in a searchable public catalog.
- eBay: direct publishing works after the supported business setup; a regular account can use an extension-filled draft.
- Facebook Marketplace: the Chrome helper fills supported fields and photos; the seller reviews and clicks Publish.
- OfferUp: Ahlam prepares the listing for a phone-app handoff on Max and Ultimate.
- Craigslist, Car-Part.com/URG, and DoorDash are coming soon. Do not demonstrate or sell them as live.

### Minute 8–9: show the operating system

Show that Ahlam also tracks:

- listings by draft, posted, and sold;
- buyer views and inquiries;
- conversations by open, deal, and closed;
- yard location, barcode, and printed labels;
- VIN- or part-based interchange assistance;
- team roles and an English/Spanish interface.

### Minute 9–10: close with a measured pilot

> Let us use the free month on real inventory. We will agree on the first ten listings, record how long the work takes, and review views, inquiries, deals, and sold revenue after 30 days. If it does not save time or create enough buyer activity to justify the plan, do not keep it.

Then book the activation session while the owner is present. Do not ask for a card during the free first month.

## The result story owners want to hear

Use the shop's own numbers, not invented case-study numbers.

> Growth costs $100 per month after the free month. If one additional part sale produces more than $100 in gross profit, that one sale can cover the software cost. If your average gross profit is $150, one additional sale leaves $50 after the Growth fee, before marketplace fees, shipping, labor, returns, taxes, and other overhead. Ahlam cannot guarantee that sale; the 30-day pilot tells us whether the extra exposure and faster listing workflow produce it for your yard.

Open `/demo#calculator`, enter the owner's real average gross profit, choose the plan, and let the calculator show the break-even number.

## Objection handling

### “I already use Car-Part.com.”

> Keep it. Ahlam is not asking you to replace a wholesale channel that already works. The value is faster intake, pricing and listing preparation, your public storefront, and extra retail reach. Car-Part.com/URG integration is still coming soon, so I will not pretend it is live today.

### “I already sell on eBay or Facebook.”

> Good—that means you already know there are buyers there. The question is how much of your inventory never makes it online because repeating the same research and typing takes too long. Ahlam creates one reviewed catalog and prepares the right path for each channel.

### “AI makes mistakes.”

> It can, which is why Ahlam does not remove the seller from the process. It gives you a starting draft, confidence and pricing help; you verify the part, fitment, condition, photos, and price before publishing.

### “I am not good with computers.”

> Take the photos and review the card. That is the main loop. I will set up the first vehicle with you and leave only after you have done the same process yourself.

### “I do not have time.”

> That is the problem we are measuring. Give me one vehicle and ten minutes. We will compare that with the time it takes you to create the same listings today.

### “Will it post everywhere automatically?”

> No. eBay can publish directly after the supported business setup. The Chrome helper can fill a regular eBay draft and the Facebook form. OfferUp uses a prepared phone handoff. You review and make the final publish decision.

### “Will it definitely increase my sales?”

> I cannot honestly guarantee a sale. I can show you that more verified parts can be listed in more places, and Ahlam will measure views, inquiries, deals, and sold revenue so we can judge the result with your own numbers.

## Do and do not say

Say:

- “Get more of your inventory online.”
- “Give each approved part more chances to be found.”
- “A starting price based on market evidence that you can edit.”
- “The seller reviews before publishing.”
- “We will measure the 30-day pilot.”

Do not say:

- “Ahlam guarantees more sales or a specific revenue increase.”
- “The AI is always accurate.”
- “Facebook or OfferUp publishes unattended.”
- “Car-Part.com, Craigslist, or DoorDash is live.”
- “Revenue estimate means money earned.” In analytics, it is the current asking value of listed inventory; sold revenue is separate.
- “Custom-domain website is included with every plan.” It is an Ultimate feature; every shop can have a public Ahlam storefront.

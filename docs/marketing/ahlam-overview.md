# Ahlam: Product Overview

Ahlam is an AI-powered platform for auto salvage yards and parts shops. It packages three services into one:

1. **A modernized version of Car-Part.com and Goldticket**: a B2B marketplace with an AI scanner built in.
2. **Digital advertising** through Facebook.
3. **A personalized shop website** with inventory that is easy to manage.

## How the AI scanner works

Ahlam lets you photograph a vehicle, and the AI breaks it down into individual parts, assigning each one a price and a condition grade.

**Vehicle identification.** The scanner reads the VIN to pull the year, make, model, trim, and body type, along with details like the engine type. It also returns the interchange type for each part, so buyers know which other vehicles it fits.

**Pricing.** Prices are based on live market data from Facebook Marketplace, OfferUp, eBay, and other marketplaces, so every part is listed at a reasonable, competitive price.

**Condition grading.** Condition is based on visible damage. A dent or paint chip on a hood, for example, lowers its grade. There are three grades, A through C, and the grade feeds directly into the price.

**Speed.** The entire process, from photos to priced inventory, takes about one minute.

Once you review the output and click **Save all parts**, the parts go to two places at once: the B2B marketplace and your own B2C website.

## B2B marketplace

The B2B marketplace is a modernized version of Car-Part.com and Goldticket. It is where the AI scanner lives and where shops do business with each other. You can:

- See other shops' inventory, sorted by preferences such as location and price.
- Message shops, make inquiries, and negotiate deals.
- Find nearby inventory, since everything is organized by proximity.

## B2C website and inventory management

Every saved part also lands on your personalized website, where inventory becomes extremely manageable. When a part sells, click **Mark as sold** and the inventory updates automatically, so what is listed always matches what is on the lot.

## AI deal agent

Your B2C website includes an AI agent that handles deals for you. The admin sets a discount benchmark, and some shops allow 5% to 10%. If a door is priced at $600 and a customer wants to negotiate, the agent works within that range on your behalf.

This suits customers who would rather not haggle with the owner face to face and prefer to close the deal online. When a deal goes through, you get a notification through your connected Gmail account, and the customer can show up with a receipt.

## Listing extension

The Ahlam browser extension takes a part or vehicle description, along with its pricing, and carries it into a listing on Facebook Marketplace or eBay. Instead of building each listing from scratch, the extension fills it in for you. It is simple, quick, and gets the job done faster than doing it by hand.

## Why shops use it

- **Confident pricing without the manager.** A sales associate can rely on Ahlam for solid numbers on parts and vehicles instead of checking with the manager every time.
- **One workflow, three channels.** Scan once and the parts reach the B2B marketplace, your website, and, through the extension, Facebook Marketplace and eBay.
- **Inventory that stays accurate.** Mark as sold keeps every channel current.
- **Deals that close themselves.** The AI agent negotiates within your limits while you focus on the yard.

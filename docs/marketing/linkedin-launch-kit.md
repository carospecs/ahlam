# Ahlam LinkedIn Launch Kit

This kit turns the team-approved [Ahlam product overview](ahlam-overview.md) into an evergreen company campaign plus a daily client-inventory spotlight. Founders control the LinkedIn connection and can pause automatic publishing at any time. Do not add claims, customer names, savings, or performance numbers that have not been verified.

## Brand direction

- Audience: independent auto dismantlers, salvage yards, and used-parts teams.
- Voice: direct, practical, respectful, and specific. Speak like a partner who has spent time in the yard, not like an enterprise software company.
- Core palette: midnight navy `#101A2C`, ink navy `#14233B`, cream `#F4EFE4`, sand `#D3B484`, and signal red `#D8392E` only as a small accent.
- Main campaign visual: [ahlam-three-promises-linkedin.png](assets/ahlam-three-promises-linkedin.png).
- Core message: We build the website. We help market the inventory. We help the yard price and cross-list it.
- Avoid: hype, invented results, generic AI language, third-party marketplace logos, and promises that a listing will publish without the seller's final review.

The LinkedIn worker rotates across marketing-enabled client sites. It chooses only active inventory with a public photo, creates one fact-checked draft for the day, and records the resulting LinkedIn URL. The client Facebook queue remains separate on Monday and Friday at 9:00 AM Pacific.

## Daily spotlight safeguards

- Use only active inventory with a public client storefront and photo.
- Rotate the least recently featured shop first, then prefer unused inventory.
- Never invent condition, savings, availability, performance numbers, or scan-time claims.
- Credit the shop and link to its storefront; the post speaks as Ahlam, not as the shop.
- Create at most one LinkedIn slot per Pacific calendar day.
- Keep automatic publishing disabled until the Ahlam Page connection is approved and tested.
- Lock a draft while publishing so a timeout or double click cannot create a duplicate.
- Pause and require reconnection if LinkedIn expires or revokes the authorization.

## Post 2: The website should reflect the real yard

**Caption**

A salvage-yard website is useful only when it shows what the yard actually has.

Ahlam connects a dismantler's working inventory to a customer-facing storefront. When an approved part or vehicle becomes active, it can appear on the yard's own site with the photo, price, fitment, and contact information customers need.

No duplicate catalog to maintain. No stale spreadsheet for the customer to guess from.

We are testing every client storefront as a buyer would: searching for a real part, opening the details, and making sure the seller can be reached.

## Post 3: Marketing from inventory that already exists

**Caption**

The best marketing idea is often already sitting in the yard.

A complete vehicle, a hard-to-find OEM part, or a fresh arrival can become useful content without asking the shop owner to write a post from scratch.

Ahlam's review queue turns real inventory facts into a ready-to-check draft. The shop or our team can edit it, open the destination, and confirm the final post.

The important part is not posting more noise. It is showing buyers what is genuinely available.

## Post 4: Pricing help without taking control away

**Caption**

Pricing a used auto part is not one clean database lookup.

Condition, fitment, damage, demand, and comparable listings all matter. The person at the yard still knows the part best.

Ahlam is designed to do the repetitive research and prepare a starting point. The seller can review the condition, adjust the price, and approve the listing before it goes anywhere.

Good automation should reduce the typing while keeping the important decisions visible.

## Approval checklist

Before publishing any post:

- Confirm every inventory item is still active and actually belongs to the named client.
- Get client approval before using its name, photos, address, or testimonial.
- Remove VINs, customer messages, account details, and private dashboard data.
- Check prices and availability on the day of publishing.
- Confirm Ahlam has permission to publicly name the featured client and use its public inventory photo.
- For manually published campaign posts, have a founder make the final publish decision.
- Record the post URL and date after it is live.

## Do not spend early selling time here

- LKQ South Texas and LKQ Pick Your Part are national corporate operations. A local owner cannot approve Ahlam.
- Houston Auto Recyclers is primarily a self-service operation. It is less likely to create one online listing per pulled part.
- Visit Auto Parts at 11414 Cora St is listed as open 24 hours, but recent public reviews include serious product and payment complaints. It is the only obvious after-6 option found, but it would be a risky first Ahlam reference customer.

## Phone opener for a 6:10 PM appointment

Use the complete phone, in-person, Spanish, objection, and closing scripts in [sales-demo-playbook.md](./sales-demo-playbook.md). Show the customer-facing page at `https://ahlam.io/demo` after it is deployed.

> Hi, this is Mohammad. I am in North Houston and my co-founder Andy and I are building Ahlam for independent used-parts yards. I want to show you something on one of your own vehicles: take a few photos, identify the sellable parts, suggest used prices, and prepare the listings and your shop page. You review everything before anything is published. The first month is free and does not need a card. I can come by at 6:10 after you close and keep it to ten minutes. Would you or the owner be there Tuesday or Wednesday?

If they already use Car-Part.com:

> Keep it. Ahlam is not asking you to replace it. I want to show you how to get the vehicle photographed, priced, and listing-ready faster, then reach the buyers you are missing on eBay, Facebook, and your own shop page.

If they say they are not good with computers:

> That is exactly the problem we built it for. You take the photos. Ahlam prepares the work. You only review it before it goes live.

## Qualify before using the car

Ask four questions on the phone:

1. Do you dismantle vehicles and sell the parts, or mainly buy cars for scrap?
2. About how many vehicles do you process in a normal month?
3. Do you list parts online now? If not, is time the reason?
4. Can the owner or person who decides on software watch a ten-minute demo?

A prospect is worth an evening appointment when it sells parts, processes at least several vehicles a month, has an online-listing problem, and the decision-maker will attend.

## Demo and close

Use one real vehicle or high-value part from the yard. In ten minutes:

1. Photograph it.
2. Show identification, condition notes, and a used-price range.
3. Correct one item together so the owner sees that the shop stays in control.
4. Show the prepared listing and branded Ahlam shop page.
5. Start the free first month only if the owner agrees to load at least ten real listings in the next 24 hours.

Current product terms found in the project: the first month is the Growth plan at $0 with no card for the first 50 yards; it includes up to 10 AI car scans and unlimited manual listings. The paid Growth plan is $100/month. Do not promise unattended one-click publishing. Describe eBay/Facebook posting as a prepared or assisted workflow that the seller reviews and publishes.
